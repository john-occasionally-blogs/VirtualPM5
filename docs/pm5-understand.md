### SUMMARY
Mapped the complete real-hardware PM5 BLE pipeline in Shared/Concept2Manager.swift (all 2106 lines) plus its byte-level companion Shared/CSAFEProtocol.swift (523 lines, all parsers/builders live there) and the substitution seam in Shared/MockConcept2Manager.swift (360 lines). The manager is a private-init singleton (Concept2Manager.shared) with no protocol seam; the mock is a sibling singleton that bypasses ALL parsing/CSAFE code by posting the same NotificationCenter dict and writing @Published properties directly — meaning today's mock exercises zero bytes of the real pipeline. The app subscribes to C2 chars 0x0031/0x0032/0x0033/0x0035/0x0039/0x003A plus FTMS 0x2AD1 and 2AD9 indications, writes CSAFE only to CE060021, and parses responses off CE060022 with a fragment accumulator, whole-frame duplicate dropper, and a 0.15s read-fallback. All parse code is byte-exact and pure ([UInt8] in, struct out) in CSAFEProtocol.swift with golden spec vectors — ideal for an emulator to target — but every parser entry point in the manager is private and dispatches on characteristic.uuid inside peripheral(_:didUpdateValueFor:), so a virtual-PM5 transport needs either a small internal ingest(uuid:data:) dispatcher added, or CBMutableCharacteristic-based delegate invocation. PM5 state is modeled from General Status byte 8 (PM5WorkoutState 0-13, isComplete = workoutEnd|workoutLogged) and drives terminate-before-reprogram, a 12s fresh-piece gate, and completion latching. There is no 8s/3s teardown chain in this file; the timing constants that exist are catalogued below. No #if os() gating exists anywhere in the three files (they compile into iOS/tvOS/watchOS/Tests targets); the only platform gate is in WorkoutSession.swift (#if os(iOS) || os(tvOS)) around who starts scanning.

### 4a. CSAFE layer — frame codec, accumulator, chunking
CSAFEFrameCodec (CSAFEProtocol.swift:22-89): standard frame = 0xF1 <stuffed(contents + XOR checksum)> 0xF2. Checksum = XOR over UNstuffed contents (excludes flags and itself). Stuffing 0xF0→F3 00, 0xF1→F3 01, 0xF2→F3 02, 0xF3→F3 03 (34-46); unstuff rejects escape values >0x03 (49-65). buildFrame (68-72); decodeFrame validates flags+stuffing+checksum, returns unstuffed contents (83-89). Chunking: bleWriteChunkSize=20 (line 28), chunked() splits frames into ≤20-byte ATT writes (75-79); PM reassembles by frame flags. CSAFEResponseAccumulator (95-115): stateful per-byte scan across notifications — 0xF1 RESYNCS (drops partial buffer), 0xF2 closes and emits a frame, bytes outside a frame discarded. writeFrame (Concept2Manager.swift:1057-1092): hex-logs to @Published lastTxFrameHex, writes each chunk; multi-chunk frames use .withResponse when supported (chunk-order guarantee), single-chunk prefers .withoutResponse (1063-1068). Read-fallback: after every write, if Rx notify not yet confirmed AND Rx has .read property, schedule peripheral.readValue(for: rx) at +0.15s (1076-1091); the fallback is permanently disabled once any genuine notification is proven (rxNotifyConfirmed latch, 2059-2070 — SC-wcxq: on notify-only firmware, ANY Rx delivery confirms; on readable firmware only a delivery landing while the fallback is still pending counts).

### 6b. Injection seams — where a byte-faithful virtual-PM5 transport must plug in
INBOUND (notifications → parsers): the single funnel is peripheral(_:didUpdateValueFor:error:) (Concept2Manager.swift:2051-2088). Its body dispatches ONLY on characteristic.uuid + characteristic.value and NEVER touches the peripheral parameter — but all downstream per-characteristic parsers are PRIVATE (parseRowerData 1373, parsePrimaryData 1400, parseSecondaryData 1474, parseAdditionalStatus2 1505, parseStrokeData 1518, parseFitnessData 1529, handleCSAFEResponseFrame 1097, handleEndOfWorkoutSummary 1169, handleAdditionalEndSummary 1225). Two viable seams: (A) add a small internal `ingest(uuid: CBUUID, data: Data)` that mirrors the didUpdateValueFor body — minimal, keeps 100% of parse/state/gate code live (fresh-piece gate, duplicate dropper, accumulator, pm5WorkoutState, retry paths all sit inside these private funcs); or (B) invoke the delegate method directly with a CBMutableCharacteristic carrying the target CBUUID and value (CBCharacteristic itself cannot be instantiated; the unused peripheral param can be any CBPeripheral reference — but CBPeripheral also has no public initializer, so B needs an ObjC-runtime allocation hack; A is the honest seam). OUTBOUND (app's CSAFE programming frames → emulator): everything funnels through private writeFrame(_:to:using:label:) (1057-1092), which requires non-nil connectedPeripheral + csafeTxCharacteristic — and EVERY CSAFE-sending code path (terminate-stale 625-635, retry chain 799-816, segment GoIdle 1327-1336, gate-timeout terminate 156-167, status probe 1342) guards on `!isMockMode` and/or `connectedPeripheral != nil`/`csafeTxCharacteristic != nil`, so with today's mock they ALL silently no-op. ### RIRT round lifecycle (LOG15) — the exact scenario to emulate
One round = arm → armed-idle → row → complete → log → teardown → re-arm. Arm (rest→work boundary, or pre-arm ~10 s after previous row): app sends TERMINATE_STALE_PIECE `F1 76 04 13 02 01 02 60 F2` (SETPMCFG 0x76 wrapping PM_SET_SCREENSTATE 0x13, screen 0x01, value 0x02=TERMINATEWORKOUT), ack ~0.4 s with varying CSAFE-state nibble (manual `F1 08 76 01 13 6C F2`, offline, idle); 0x0031 flips workoutLogged→waitToBegin within ~1 packet but distance stays FROZEN at the old piece's 250.0 m for 1-2 more packets (app drops as 'stale'); then DISTANCE_WORKOUT_0x76_SUB500 `F1 76 18 01 01 03 03 05 80 00 00 00 FA 05 05 80 00 00 00 FA 14 01 01 13 02 01 01 6E F2` (WORKOUTTYPE=3 fixedDist, WORKOUTDURATION type 0x80 val 250, SPLITDURATION same, CONFIGURE_WORKOUT=1, SCREENSTATE 01 01=PREPARETOROWWORKOUT), written in 2 BLE chunks, acked `F1 82 76 05 01 03 05 14 13 F3 01 F2` (echoes accepted sub-cmd ids; checksum 0xF1 byte-stuffed as F3 01). 0x0031 then becomes the armed all-zero pattern `00 00 00|00 00 00|03|01|00|00|01|00 00 00|FA 00 00|80|00` (elapsed=0, dist=0, type=3, state=0 waitToBegin, duration=250 m/type 0x80, drag=0) repeating at 2 Hz for the whole rest (340 near-identical packets in the round-3→4 gap). Row start needs NO app command — PM5 self-transitions to workoutRow on flywheel motion (~4 s after round start; zero TX during the entire row). Distance counts UP (18→86→153→222→247 m in app parse), drag factor goes live (0x7A=122). At 250.0 m: workoutEnd → 0x0039 + workoutLogged ~1-2 s later → app sends SEGMENT_GO_FINISHED `F1 86 86 F2` ~9 s after workoutEnd (REJECTED by erg, `F1 98 98 F2`), GoIdle `F1 82 82 F2` +1 s, then TERMINATE + next-round program +1 s. Total re-arm-to-armed ≈ 12 s after piece end.

### SC-9ir8 even-round refusal signature + PM5-side corruption race
Round N's completion handler PRE-ARMS round N+1 ~10 s after the row ends (mid work phase). 47 ticks (~52 s) later the work-interval-complete handler tears it down: '🏋️ onWorkIntervalComplete called - showing score overlay' → 'Sending cmdGoFinished (0x86)' → '🗑️ Cleared programmed workout' → '🔄 Rowing segment deactivated: rowed 0m this round, cumulative 250m' (lines 50766-50771 for R2, 64690-64693 for R4). The erg REJECTS the GoFinished (`F1 11 11 F2` reject/ready for R2, `F1 92 92 F2` reject/idle for R4) so its armed 250 m program survives — but the APP's segment is dead. At the next round start the refusal signature fires: '🚣 🔄 armRowingSegment: round 2 already armed (highest: 2)' (55031) / 'round 4 already armed (highest: 4)' (68936), ~4 ticks before the erg's workoutRow. The lost round rows untracked ('Remaining: 0m', no 'Segment complete', 'SC-c9fl round N metrics: split=-s meters=-'). Alternation: a round after a TRACKED round is lost; a round after a LOST round arms cleanly at rest→work and tracks (R1/R3/R5 tracked, R2/R4 lost). PM5-side race: the teardown's GoIdle is sent AFTER the next arm; when the erg ACKs it (R2: `F1 82 82 F2` ok/idle) the subsequent piece's 0x0031 workoutType flips 03→01 one packet in and its 0x0039 is degenerate — `F7 34 1A 15 58 16 00 00 00 00 0B 8F 00 00 00 7B 00 01 FF FF` = 57.2 s, 0.0 m (!), 11 spm garbage, ending HR 143 real, type 0x01, pace 0xFFFF — even though 0x0031 counted 3.5→250 m normally and the erg auto-ended at 250 m. CE060032 (17 B): [0-2] elapsed, [3-4] speed ×0.001 m/s, [5] strokeRate, [6] HR (0xFF = no strap), [7-8] current pace ×0.01 s, [9-10] avg pace — 2.0 Hz. ### CSAFE response/reject grammar the emulator must reproduce
RX status byte = frame-toggle 0x80 | prev-frame-reject 0x10 | CSAFE state nibble (1 ready, 2 idle, 5 inUse, 7 finished, 8 manual, 9 offline). Program acks echo accepted sub-command ids (`F1 82 76 05 01 03 05 14 13 F3 01 F2` — note byte-stuffing of 0xF1/0xF2 checksums as F3 01/F3 02). TERMINATE acks echo `76 01 13` with state varying by screen context (manual/offline/idle/inUse). SEGMENT_GO_FINISHED (0x86) is REJECTED by the real erg in nearly every observed state: `F1 98 98 F2` (reject/manual, from logged), `F1 92 92 F2` (reject/idle, while armed), `F1 11 11 F2` (reject/ready), `F1 19 19 F2` (reject/offline, LOG11 EMOM); programming during an active piece → `F1 99 99 F2`; interval re-program while inUse → `F1 15 1A 00 0F F2` + `F1 15 15 F2`. The PM5 also re-delivers identical response frames ('🔁 Dropping duplicate CSAFE response delivery'). An always-ACK emulator would hide all of these app code paths.
[EVIDENCE: erg-row.log 48922, 50773, 61154, 62883, 62995, 64723, 78471; erg-session-2026-07-11.log 171486-171751]
